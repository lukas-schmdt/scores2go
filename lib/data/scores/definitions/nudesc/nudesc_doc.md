# Nursing Delirium Screening Scale (Nu-DESC)

The Nu-DESC is a brief, nurse-administered delirium screening tool designed for continuous use at the bedside. It consists of five items, each rated 0–2, yielding a total score of 0–10. A score of **≥ 2 indicates delirium**.

## Scale

Each item is rated as follows:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Absent |
| 1 | Mild / intermittent |
| 2 | Present / pronounced |

## Items

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Disorientation** | Patient appears confused about time, place, or person; does not recognise surroundings or hospital staff |
| **Inappropriate behavior** | Pulls at tubes/catheters, attempts to get out of bed inappropriately, uncooperative with care |
| **Inappropriate communication** | Incoherent, disorganised or unintelligible speech; mumbling; unusual sounds |
| **Illusions / Hallucinations** | Sees or hears things that are not present |
| **Psychomotor retardation** | Slowed reaction, few or no spontaneous movements, delayed response to stimulation |

## Score Interpretation

| Total | Interpretation |
|-------|---------------|
| 0 | No delirium |
| 1 | No delirium (borderline — reassess regularly) |
| ≥ 2 | Delirium likely — clinical assessment recommended |
| ≥ 5 | Severe delirium — immediate assessment required |

:::tip Screening Frequency
The Nu-DESC is intended for **once-per-shift** assessment. Regular reassessment is key to detecting fluctuating delirium, which may be missed with single observations.
:::

## Assessment Procedure

1. Observe the patient during routine nursing care.
2. Rate each of the five items based on behavior observed **during the current shift** (not just at the time of assessment).
3. Sum all item scores.
4. A total score of **≥ 2** warrants further evaluation and documentation; notify the treating physician.

:::info Hypoactive Delirium
The **psychomotor retardation** item specifically targets hypoactive delirium, which is frequently missed by tools focusing solely on agitation. Up to 50% of ICU delirium is hypoactive or mixed.
:::

## Psychometric Properties

- Sensitivity: **85.7%** | Specificity: **86.7%** (vs. DSM-IV criteria)
- Validated in postoperative and medical/surgical ward patients
- High inter-rater reliability among nurses
