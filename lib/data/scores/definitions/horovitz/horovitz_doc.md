# Horovitz Quotient (PaO₂ / FiO₂ Ratio)

The Horovitz quotient is the ratio of arterial oxygen partial pressure (PaO₂) to the fraction of inspired oxygen (FiO₂). It is the standard measure of oxygenation efficiency and forms the basis of the ARDS Berlin Definition severity classification.

## Formula

```text
Horovitz Quotient = PaO₂ (mmHg) / FiO₂ (fraction)
```

**Example:** PaO₂ = 80 mmHg, FiO₂ = 40 % → 80 / 0.40 = **200 mmHg**

## Interpretation

| Quotient (mmHg) | Classification |
|-----------------|----------------|
| ≥ 400 | Normal oxygenation |
| 300–399 | Mild hypoxemia |
| 200–299 | Mild ARDS (Berlin Definition) |
| 100–199 | Moderate ARDS (Berlin Definition) |
| < 100 | Severe ARDS (Berlin Definition) |

## ARDS Berlin Definition (2012)

The Berlin Definition requires, in addition to the P/F ratio threshold, all of the following criteria to be met:

- **Timing:** acute onset within 1 week of a known clinical insult or new/worsening respiratory symptoms
- **Chest imaging:** bilateral opacities not fully explained by effusions, collapse, or nodules
- **Origin of oedema:** respiratory failure not fully explained by cardiac failure or fluid overload
- **PEEP:** ≥ 5 cmH₂O for all severity categories

:::note PEEP Correction
The Horovitz quotient is measured **on the current ventilator settings** including PEEP. Without PEEP ≥ 5 cmH₂O the ARDS classification cannot be applied per Berlin criteria.
:::

## Clinical Notes

- The ratio should be obtained from an **arterial blood gas** (not SpO₂-derived estimates).
- FiO₂ must reflect the **actual delivered** concentration — verify on the ventilator or high-flow device.
- Serial measurements track the **response to interventions** (prone positioning, PEEP titration, recruitment manoeuvres).
- The **SpO₂/FiO₂ (SF) ratio** is a non-invasive surrogate: SF ≈ 57 + 0.61 × PF (can be used when ABG is unavailable).
