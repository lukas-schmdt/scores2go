SAPS 3

Simplified Acute Physiology Score 3 predicts in-hospital mortality using 20 variables collected at ICU admission. Validated in 19,577 patients across 35 countries.

Moreno et al. 2005 Popularity 78

Overview

SAPS 3 is organised into three boxes collecting information available at the time of ICU admission. The total score ranges from 0 to 217 points, which is converted to a predicted in-hospital mortality probability via a logistic regression equation. Unlike APACHE II, SAPS 3 offers seven region-specific equations to correct for international differences in case mix and hospital structure.

Mortality Formula

logit = −32.6659 + ln(SAPS 3 score + 1) × 7.3068

Predicted mortality = e^logit / (1 + e^logit)

Seven region-specific equations (Europe, Americas, etc.) are available for improved local calibration.
